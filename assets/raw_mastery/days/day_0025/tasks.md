### 1. BP25：图表小作文首段：题目改写与总体趋势概览（Paraphrase + Macro Overview）
- **我这一段要表达什么**：我这一段想写雅思 Task 1 或数据报告的第一段：用同义句法改写图表标题，并用两句话高度凝练全图最核心的 2 个宏观趋势（极值、反超拐点或主次格局），绝不堆砌零碎数字。
- **逐句构建公式**：
  - S1 [图表命题同义改写 Prompt Paraphrase]：The line graph / bar chart compares [Variable/Metric] across [Categories/Regions] over a [N]-year period from [Year A] to [Year B].
  - S2 [宏观趋势 1：总体走向与反超 Overview Trend 1]：Overall, while [Category A and B] experienced a steady upward trajectory, [Category C] declined markedly, leading to a reversal in dominance around [Mid-Year].
  - S3 [宏观趋势 2：极值或差距特征 Overview Trend 2]：Notably, [Category A] registered the most dramatic growth throughout the period, ultimately overtaking all other sectors to become the primary contributor.
- **病段反例**：
  > ❌ *The chart shows the energy consumption from 2000 to 2025. We can see that solar energy went up a lot from 5% to 35%, and coal went down from 60% to 25%. Wind also went up.*
- **病灶诊断**：① 词汇低幼（went up a lot, went down）；② 把具体百分比过早塞进了 Overview，导致后面细节段无话可写。
- **母语级标杆段**：
  > ✅ **The line graph compares the proportion of electricity generated from four fuel sources across three industrial economies between 2000 and 2025. Overall, renewable sources—particularly solar and wind power—followed a pronounced upward trajectory over the 25-year span, whereas coal reliance contracted steadily. Most notably, clean energy generation overtook fossil-fuel consumption around 2018, shifting from a marginal supplement to the dominant power supply by the end of the period.**

### 2. 今日逻辑衔接引擎：L025 具身智能感知-决策-控制闭环与 Sim-to-Real 论证规范（Embodied AI Closed-Loop Logic）
- **核心法则**：论述具身智能（Embodied AI）与机器人策略时，严禁将其等同于静态图像识别。必须突出**闭环反馈（Closed-loop feedback）**、**分布外状态累积偏移（Covariate shift in sequential decision-making）**、**接触动力学不确定性（Contact-rich dynamics）**以及**机载实时推理延迟约束（Onboard real-time latency）**。
- **结构公式**：`Unlike static perception where errors remain isolated, closed-loop manipulation compounds small action deviations into out-of-distribution states. -> To maintain stability under contact uncertainty, our policy [Mechanism].`
- **正误对比**：
  - ❌ *The robot sees the image from the camera and predicts the action to pick up the cup accurately.*
  - ✅ *Unlike static visual recognition where prediction errors remain isolated across samples, closed-loop robotic manipulation compounds minor trajectory deviations into unfamiliar contact states. To prevent this covariate drift during precision assembly, our visuomotor policy conditions short-horizon action chunks on real-time wrist force-torque feedback.*
